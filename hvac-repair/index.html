Signs You Need HVAC Repair in Odessa

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • The system short-cycling on and off without reaching the set temperature
  • Unfamiliar sounds from the equipment, like squealing, grinding, or banging
  • A humming outdoor unit that never kicks on
  • Warm air from the vents in cooling mode, or cool air in heating mode
  • Higher energy bills with no improvement in comfort
  • Puddles near the furnace or air handler from a blocked drain line
  • A circuit breaker that keeps tripping when the system starts
  • Barely any air coming out of the vents

Maintenance Tips for HVAC Repair

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Trim shrubs and clear leaves and grass away from the outdoor unit so the coil gets full airflow
  • Give the system a rest during extreme weather by setting the thermostat a few degrees closer to outdoors
  • Replace the air filter every one to three months to keep airflow strong across the blower and coil
  • Have the system professionally serviced twice a year, once before summer and once before winter
  • Keep supply and return registers open and unblocked in every room
  • Run a vinegar flush through the condensate line each spring to prevent clogs and float switch trips
  • Do not ignore new sounds; catching a noisy motor before it locks up saves real money
